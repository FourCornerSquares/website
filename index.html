FAQ

Do You Respect MAP and Suggested Pricing?

Yes. We never list below your minimum advertised price, and we follow the pricing guidelines you share with us. When your pricing changes, send us the update and we adjust our listings.

Where Do You Buy Your Inventory?

Only directly from the brand or from its authorized distributors, with an invoice for every purchase. We never source from liquidators, retail stores or other resellers.

Which Marketplaces Do You Sell On?

Amazon.ca and Amazon.com. Every order is fulfilled by Amazon, so your customers get fast Prime delivery and Amazon's customer service.

How Do You Choose Which Products to Order?

We review your full catalogue, order the products that fit Amazon best, then reorder based on real sales so your listings stay in stock.

How Do You Pay?

Prepaid, by credit card or bank transfer. There are no payment terms to manage and nothing to chase.

Do You Follow Brand Channel Restrictions?

Yes. Tell us about any brand or product line that can't be sold on Amazon, and we won't order it.

Do You Need Exclusivity?

No. We work alongside your existing retail and online channels and follow any channel or pricing guidelines you have. For brands that want a single seller on Amazon, we can build an exclusive arrangement around your goals, so the brand grows and stays protected over the long term.

What Do You Need to Manage Our Brand Registry?

A registered or pending trademark for your brand. You enroll as the owner and add us as your agent, and you can change or remove our access at any time.
